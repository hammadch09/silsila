LAYER TWO · WHY IT KEEPS HAPPENING

Four things go wrong, and they go wrong in order.

01

The goal is too big to start today.

“Become a backend engineer” is not something you can do at 9pm on a Tuesday. So the day passes, and the goal is exactly where you left it.

02

Nobody tells you what today is.

Deciding what to work on costs more than the work. Most evenings the only decision made is what to skip.

03

Nobody looks at what you made.

You finish something and it goes nowhere. No grade, no correction, no idea if it was any good. Work without feedback stops feeling like progress.

04

So nothing accumulates.

Four years of scattered effort and no single place that shows it. That is the blank CV — not a talent problem, four missing pieces of scaffolding.

Silsila exists to fix all four at once: it cuts the goal into days, sends today's, grades what you send back, and keeps every piece in one place.

HOW IT WORKS

A plan that runs itself,
and checks on you.

01

It builds the plan.

You say what you are after. It asks your semester, your level and the hours you actually have, then writes three months down to individual days.

02

It sends one task a day.

Thirty to forty-five minutes, on WhatsApp. Small enough that a semester project cannot justify skipping it.

03

It grades what you send back.

Not a tick. A grade against the task’s own criteria — one thing you did well, one thing to fix. For most students it is the first time anyone has looked.

04

You can ask it anything.

Stuck at 11pm with nobody to ask is the moment people quit. It knows your plan and where you are in it.

05

It piles up.

Every graded submission adds to a profile you can send an employer. Finish one three-month plan, start the next.
